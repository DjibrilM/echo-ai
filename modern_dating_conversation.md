## 💡 Echo AI Tips
* **Lightweight Descriptions**: Parler-TTS performs best with concise scene descriptions (e.g., `A woman in a podcast environment`) rather than long adjective lists.
* **Per-Speaker Sidebar**: In Echo AI, click on each Speaker card to set their voice description directly in the sidebar drawer.
* **Pause Tags**: The `[pause 0.3s]` tags provide natural comedic pauses between conversational beats.
