## 💡 How To Make Dialogue Sound Human in Echo AI

1. **Reaction & Tone Cues**:
   * You can write `[hmm]` for natural thoughtful pauses and introspective reactions.
   * Use tone modifiers like `[whisper]`, `[yell]`, and `[raise]` for pointed vocal modulation.
2. **Natural Breathing vs `[pause]` Cuts**:
   * Use ellipses `...` or em-dashes `—` inside sentences. This preserves continuous melody, natural breath intake, and pitch cadence without chopping the audio into disconnected pieces.
   * Echo AI fills deliberate pauses with natural studio acoustic room air rather than unnatural dead digital silence.
3. **CosyVoice 3 vs Parler-TTS Prompts**:
   * **CosyVoice 3**: Identity is cloned from the **reference audio**; the prompt is an **Acting Instruction** (`Speaking with energetic sarcasm`, `Whispering softly`).
   * **Parler-TTS**: The prompt sets both the **persona and studio environment** (`A Black American woman in a podcast studio`).
4. **Higher Precision & Less Quantized Models**:
   * Unquantized **FP16** and **FP32** models (and **Parler-TTS Large 2.2B**) provide richer acoustic detail and smoother emotional range.
